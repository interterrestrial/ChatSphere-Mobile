# ChatSphere

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Purpose

ChatSphere is a portfolio demo of one-to-one messaging on the web and Android. Someone trying the demo should be able to find a person, start a conversation, and exchange messages across devices.

## Product behavior

Email or Google sign-in leads to username setup and an inbox. People can search for an account, open a direct conversation, and exchange messages. Gemini can suggest an editable reply or summarize a thread on request. The web experience establishes the visual language for Android.
